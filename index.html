🧠 Final Inflation Investigation

Answer individually after the game:

  1. If your budget stayed at $100 but food prices increased, did your purchasing power increase or decrease? Explain.
  2. Why did some teams have to sacrifice items even though they had the same budget?
  3. If your family's income increased by 5% but Thanksgiving prices increased by 20%, would your family be better off? Why or why not?
  4. Is inflation necessarily a problem if everyone's income increases at the same rate as prices? Explain.
Teacher note: Prices shown are illustrative game prices, not official historical retail data. This game is designed to simulate price increases. Event cards are randomized and only apply once drawn. The transport event adds $1 per item currently in the cart; unexpected guests add a $15 food requirement (or teams may explain a reasonable alternative).
